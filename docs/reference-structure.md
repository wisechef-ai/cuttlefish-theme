# Terminal Theme Background-Pattern Report

Measurements are visual estimates. Let `g` = one monospace glyph height (roughly 16–18 px in these renders). “Lit area” means visibly non-ground particle/core/halo area, not the dark ground beneath a translucent panel.

## Direction P — Ghost Camo (`img_ebfcd43238c8.jpeg`)

- **Ground and coverage:** Blue-black/navy, approximately `#030A13`–`#050D18` (shadow pockets near `#02070E`), present under essentially the full frame (~100% base coverage). Dark ground visually occupies ~88–95% because the particle field is sparse.
- **Lit elements:** Isolated 1–2 px points (`~0.06–0.12g`), occasional 2–4 px points (`~0.12–0.25g`). Overall visible lit area ~5–12%; loose clouds locally reach ~15–30%. This is primarily single-point scatter with small clusters, not continuous bands.
- **Edges/halo:** Point cores are crisp/hard, with ~0–1 px transition. Brighter cyan/white/pink points have a restrained 1–3 px halo (`~0.06–0.19g`). Cloud boundaries are soft only because point density tapers.
- **Arrangement:** Irregular camouflage/nebula field: broad horizontal-to-diagonal swaths, no repeating grid or fixed stripe period. Sparse text-heavy zones; denser side margins and open regions. Cloud widths range roughly `5–35g`.
- **Colours:** About 8–10 visible families: ground `#030A13`; dim teal `#008B9A`; bright cyan `#00C7D8`; blue `#124B78`/`#2C75A8`; pale blue-white `#A7D7E8`/`#D6E8EE`; lavender `#A99AD8`; pale violet `#D7B4E8`; occasional pink `#D96DBA`; rare gold `#D8B34A`.
- **Text versus margins:** Pattern continues behind text, but dots are dimmer/sparser and the ground dominates there. Empty margins show more saturated, larger, higher-contrast clouds; no hard pattern-free text knockout.
- **Bottom rule/input:** A crisp multicolour dotted separator, ~1–2 px (`~0.06–0.12g`), runs nearly full width above the dark segmented status strip. Dots/short segments are spaced about 3–6 px (`~0.2–0.4g`). The input row is another dark translucent band below it, ending in a rounded multicolour dotted bottom border; not a single solid line.

## Direction L — Zebra Static (`img_85f91190dbe8.jpeg`)

- **Ground and coverage:** Near-black blue-black, approximately `#0B0B10`, full-canvas base (~100%). Visible dots/stripes cover roughly ~10–25% of area, leaving substantial ground between them.
- **Lit elements:** Mostly ~1 px dots (`~0.06g`), with small point clusters. Dots are spaced ~3–4 px (`~0.2–0.25g`). They aggregate into diagonal stripe bands ~6–8 px wide (`~0.4–0.5g`), with ~12–14 px period (`~0.75–0.9g`). Lit fraction is highest within stripes, not broad blotches.
- **Edges/halo:** Dot cores remain readable but carry a slight soft halo/bloom; stripe edges are density-defined rather than hard geometric edges.
- **Arrangement:** Uniform, repeated diagonal zebra bands at approximately 45° (top-left to bottom-right), continuous across the frame and behind panels. This is the most regular/patterned image; it is not random scatter.
- **Colours:** Roughly 5–7 tones: ground `#0B0B10`; cyan `#22D3EE`; magenta/purple `#C084FC`/`#B14DFF`; orange/amber `#F5A623`; green `#3DDC84`; occasional pale/white highlights. Hues alternate/intermix along the stripes.
- **Text versus margins:** The same field runs edge-to-edge behind text and margins. Text is a high-contrast foreground; there is no clearly flatter text-only zone.
- **Bottom rule/input:** A horizontal dotted rule uses the same ~1 px dots and ~3 px spacing (`~0.06g` and `~0.2g`) above the dark status bar. The bottom input is a dark row over the continuing diagonal field; the bottom border is also dotted/multicolour rather than a solid single-colour line.

## Direction G — Pearl Scatter (`img_5ac488fc7da8.jpeg`)

- **Ground and coverage:** Very dark blue-black, approximately `#0B0B14`, base coverage ~100%. Ground is most visible in the sparse left/top regions; bright particle bands occupy roughly the right ~60% of width.
- **Lit elements:** Pearl cores range ~0.15–0.5g in diameter. Bright bands have approximately one pearl per `~0.8g`; sparse regions may separate pearls by ~3g. The bright diagonal bands account for roughly ~20–35% of frame area, with strong cores nearer ~10–20%.
- **Edges/halo:** Soft circular cores, each with diffuse glow extending ~0.3–0.5g beyond the core. No hard pixel-block edge dominates.
- **Arrangement:** Organic diagonal flow from upper-left toward lower-right. Clusters form luminous bands with a sparse/dark corner; not grid-aligned. Density is intentionally zoned: substantially denser on the right and through central diagonal streams.
- **Colours:** About 6–8 tones: ground `#0B0B14`; cyan `#3FE0E8`; magenta/pink `#E85FB0`; gold/orange `#F2C066`; white `#FFFFFF`; violet `#9B6FE8`; dim intermediate blends. White/gold pearls are sparse highlights.
- **Text versus margins:** Panels use dark translucent overlays and thin multicolour borders. The field remains faintly visible behind text, but the brightest clusters are mainly in margins/open areas; text zones are flatter/darker for legibility.
- **Bottom rule/input:** A thin glowing horizontal rule (~0.1g) separates the response panel from the status bar and reads as a continuous rainbow/multicolour line rather than a dotted strip. The input is a dark bottom row with a `>` prompt; its border carries the same glow.

## Direction 0 — Ember Current (`img_62eeece49e1a.jpeg`)

- **Ground and coverage:** Near-black charcoal with a slight blue/violet cast, approximately `#0A0A0F`–`#0D0D12`, covering ~100% of the frame.
- **Lit elements:** Individual particles are mostly 1–2 px (`~0.06–0.12g`). Dense swirling patches and diagonal currents make roughly ~10–20% of the canvas visibly lit, with local clusters much denser. Elements are points and short point clusters, not long solid bands.
- **Edges/halo:** Relatively soft particle edges; brighter orange/magenta points have a visible but narrow glow. Halo is typically ~0.1–0.3g, with no broad fog.
- **Arrangement:** Flowing diagonal currents from upper-left toward lower-right, with branching tendrils and variable-width clusters. The structure is wave/stream-like, not grid or uniform scatter.
- **Colours:** Warm-dominant palette of ~7 tones: ground `#0A0A0F`; ember orange `#FF6A00`/`#FF8C1A`; magenta `#FF2D9B`; cyan `#19D3C8`; green `#3DDC84`; yellow `#FFD24A`; red `#FF3B3B`.
- **Text versus margins:** Pattern is present behind all text, but solid bright glyphs dominate. Exposed frame margins show the strongest multicolour dotted border and current contrast; text panels are visually quieter but not erased.
- **Bottom rule/input:** A dotted horizontal separator sits above the pill-like status bar; dots are small, hard-readable, and spaced about ~4 px (`~0.25g`). Status and input rows are approximately one glyph-height each in the render, dark-filled, with the input prompt below; the rule is multicolour rather than a single solid line.

- **Lit elements:** 1–2 px specks (`~0.06–0.12g`); dense cores have ~1 dot per 3–5 px and fade into sparse halos. Cloud clusters are about `5–15g` across. This is clustered point scatter with no continuous bars.

# Cross-cutting properties shared by all renders

1. **Full-frame dark base layer:** Every render starts with a nearly uniform near-black/navy ground covering approximately 98–100% of the frame. The pattern is an overlay, not a set of isolated decorative patches; a generator that recolours random cells without preserving a stable dark field will fail.
2. **Multi-scale organization:** Bright material is structured at two scales: tiny particles (usually `~0.06–0.2g`, occasionally up to `~0.5g`) and larger density envelopes/bands/clouds (`~5–35g`). It is never merely independent identically distributed coloured cells.
3. **Non-uniform density with negative space:** All fields alternate dark gaps with denser zones; spacing and brightness are correlated over many glyphs. Even the busiest pattern retains a dark ground, while sparse patterns preserve isolated points separated by several cells.
4. **Controlled softness:** Particle cores remain individually legible, but brighter points generally have a small halo and larger formations fade through density/brightness falloff. A naive hard-edged random-colour fill misses both crisp cores and soft envelopes.
5. **UI-aware suppression and bottom hierarchy:** The wallpaper continues behind text, but panels/status/input areas are darker or more opaque and the margins expose more contrast. Near the bottom, every render has an intentional horizontal divider plus dark segmented status/input rows; the divider is a designed thin rule (dotted, glowing, or segmented), never an accidental random row of cells.
